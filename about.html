About Us

Image Resizer is a free web tool with one job: make any picture the size you need — smaller dimensions, a smaller file, or a different format — without installing an app or handing your photos to a server.

The need is an everyday one. A phone photo is often 4000 pixels wide and several megabytes heavy, while the place it is going — a job application, a marketplace listing, a profile picture, a blog post — wants something far smaller. Shrinking it used to mean opening a photo editor or uploading the picture to a website and hoping it stayed private. This tool does the job in the browser, in seconds, and the picture never leaves your device.

What the tool does

  • Resize by pixels or by percent — type an exact width and height, or shrink by a percentage of the original.
  • Keep aspect ratio — on by default, so your picture never comes out stretched or squashed.
  • JPG, PNG and WebP output — pick the format that fits: JPG for photos, PNG for transparency, WebP for the smallest files.
  • Target file size — set a limit in KB or MB and the tool adjusts the quality to fit it, for JPG and WebP.
  • Private by design — all resizing happens on your own device with canvas. Nothing is uploaded, stored or shared.

Who we are

We build small, free tools that answer one question well. Image Resizer is our fifth — alongside the SpeakerCleaners tool, the White Screen test, the Phone Screen Resolution tool and the Phone Screen Test. Each one does a single job, in the browser, for free.

Our promise

The tool is free and will stay free. There are no accounts, no subscriptions, no locked features, no watermarks and no downloads. We do not collect your pictures or your data — every resize runs on your own device, and when you close the page, nothing remains.

If the tool saved you a re-upload or a slow page, share it with someone who still wrestles with oversized photos.
