Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Image Resizer is a free tool that resizes images in your browser. This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you use it.

Your images stay on your device

When you choose an image, it is opened and resized on your own device, inside your browser, using canvas. Your image is never uploaded to a server, never stored by us, and never shared with anyone. When you close the page, the image is gone. We cannot see your pictures, and we do not want to.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, sign in, or give us your name, address or phone number to use the tool. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information about visitors. The tool works without any of it.

Contact form

If you choose to write to us through the contact form, your message is sent through your own email app to us. We only use the details you write in that message to read it and reply to you. We do not share those details with anyone else, and we do not add you to any mailing list.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use cookies and does not run advertising trackers. If an analytics or advertising tool is added in the future, this policy will be updated to say so before it is used.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect basic technical information, such as IP addresses in its server logs, as part of hosting the site — that is GitHub's own practice, not ours, and we do not receive or use that information.

Children

This tool is a general-purpose utility for everyone. It does not knowingly collect information from children, because it does not collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Small wording fixes do not change what the tool does: your images stay on your device.

Questions

If you have a question about this policy, please reach out through the contact page.
