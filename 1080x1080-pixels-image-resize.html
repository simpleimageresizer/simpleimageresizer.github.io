What is a 1080 × 1080 image?

1080 × 1080 is the Instagram square post size — the size every square photo on Instagram is shown at. It is also used for square Facebook posts and profile pictures.

A 1080 × 1080 image holds 1.17 megapixels. At 300 DPI it prints at about 9.1 × 9.1 cm. Because it is square, it fits profile pictures, product grids and social posts without cropping.

How to resize an image to 1080 × 1080 in 4 steps

  1. Choose your image

Which format is best for a 1080 × 1080 image?

JPG is the best default for a 1080 × 1080 social image — small files and good quality for photos. Pick PNG if the image has text or a logo with sharp edges, and WebP if you want the smallest file.

Tips for a sharp 1080 × 1080 image

  • Upload the finished 1080 × 1080 image as-is — if the platform has to resize it again, the quality drops twice.
  • Turn Keep aspect ratio on if you want to keep the original shape — with it off, a square target can stretch a wide photo.
  • Start from the largest original you have — shrinking a big image to 1080 × 1080 stays sharp, but enlarging a small one will look soft.
  • For photos, JPG at the default quality is plenty for the web; only raise it if you can see the difference.

Frequently asked questions

Is 1080×1080 the Instagram post size?

Yes — Instagram shows square posts at 1080 × 1080 pixels, so resizing to exactly this size means Instagram will not crop or stretch your photo.

Will Instagram crop my 1080×1080 photo?

No. A square post at 1080 × 1080 is shown as-is. Only photos with a different shape get cropped.

Will resizing to 1080 × 1080 stretch my image?

No — when your image has a different shape, a crop box appears over the preview. Drag it to the part you want to keep, and the download is exactly 1080 × 1080 pixels with no stretching.

What file size will a 1080 × 1080 JPG be?

It depends on the photo, but a 1080 × 1080 JPG at good quality is roughly 174–348 KB. Setting a target file size will shrink it further if you need it smaller.

Can I resize to 1080 × 1080 on my phone?

Yes. The tool works in any modern browser on phones, tablets, laptops and desktops — no app to install.
